# documentos-fiscales module

Spec 25. Five routes. Records invoices issued **outside** the system and ties them to the finalized lotes that back them.

**The system registers, it does not emit**: no correlativo generation, no authorization, no tax calculation, no total in words, no PDF. Registering does **not** make the company compliant with any invoicing regime — compliance lives wherever the invoice is issued (print shop or invoicing system); this makes the export **auditable**. That is also why it works identically for an *autoimpresor* and for a print shop. The cost is double data entry; four nets catch part of it — the `UNIQUE` (duplicate number), `patron_numero` (malformed number), `validateTotalesCuadran` (incoherent sum) and `archivo_url` (compare against the scan). **None catches a mistyped amount that still adds up.**

## Access control

**None of the five routes validates `cliente_operador` and none has an enforced permission**; spec 25 also declined to create a `CONTADOR` role. So a scale operator can register **and annul** a fiscal document, and annulling removes from the listing the evidence the module exists to keep — mitigated only by design (annulling deletes nothing, leaves `anulado_por`/`anulado_en`, and the detail still returns the document). The two reads expose every invoice of the company, walkable by a sequential id. Accepted risk, recorded in the spec.

## Tables

- **`paises_config`** — one row per country, the first table that **parametrizes behaviour**: `moneda` (default for a document), `etiqueta_autorizacion` (`CAI` in Honduras, `Autorización FEL` in Guatemala, `Clave` in Costa Rica), `etiqueta_identificacion` (`RTN`, `NIT`, `RUC`), `patron_numero` and `patron_autorizacion` (`NULL` = do not validate) and `requiere_autorizacion` (compare by truthiness, it is a `TINYINT`). **There is no `cai` column, no `duca` column and no regex of Honduras anywhere in code**: Honduras is a row, and changing the rule is an `UPDATE`, not a deploy. Seeded and maintained **by hand** (no CRUD, no startup validation). A bad regex rejects valid documents or hangs the request on catastrophic backtracking; `resolvePais` wraps `new RegExp` in a `try/catch` so a malformed pattern answers 400, which does not cover backtracking. Currently **one row**: Honduras, `patron_numero` set, `patron_autorizacion` `NULL`, `requiere_autorizacion` `1` — the CAI is demanded but its shape is not checked.
- **`documentos_fiscales`** — the header. `isActive` + `motivo_anulacion`/`anulado_por`/`anulado_en` (written only by `anular`); `documento_aduanero` and `archivo_url` (written only by `completar`, once each); everything else written at creation and **never updated**. `pais_destino CHAR(2)` ISO 3166, stored so a future export-summary spec needs no DDL. `archivo_url` is a string — no upload, no storage. `fecha_emision` is a `DATE` and travels as `'YYYY-MM-DD'` (`dateStrings: ['DATE']`). Real **`UNIQUE (pais_id, numero_completo)`** (`uq_doc_numero`) — over the pair because two countries may issue the same number, and real because two documents with one number in an audit is a fine.
- **`documento_fiscal_impuesto`** — one row per tax rate, with `tarifa` as a **value**, so a new rate needs no DDL. Usually **empty** (exports are exempt; the amount sits in `importe_exento`). Written only at creation, read only by the detail, never updated, not deleted on annulment.
- **`documento_fiscal_lote`** — the bridge: many lotes per document, `cantidad` with its own `unidad_medida_id` (not necessarily the lote's unit). Only `FINALIZADO` lotes can be linked, and a lote cannot sit in two active documents.

`DocumentosFiscalesRepository` holds eleven private validators and the project's **only validation rule that does not live in its DTO**: the format of `numero_completo` is checked in the repository against `paises_config.patron_numero`, because Zod does not query the database and cannot know the country. **Do not move it to the DTO.**

## Routes

**`POST /documentos-fiscales`** — header, taxes and lotes in **one** transaction; `{ ok, msg, documento_id }`. `pais_id` optional: omitted, it resolves to the single active country; with two or more active it answers **400 asking for the field**, never choosing silently. `moneda` omitted falls back to the country's. Nine validators, in order: `resolvePais`, `validateFormatoNumero`, `validateAutorizacion` (only if `requiere_autorizacion`), `validateClienteActivo`, `validateNumeroDisponible` (readable 400 before the constraint fires; **ignores `isActive`**, like the `UNIQUE`), `validateLotesExistenYSonDelCliente`, `validateLotesFinalizados` (`finalizado_por IS NOT NULL`; does not look at `motivo_rechazo`, a finalized lote cannot be rejected), `validateLotesNoFacturados` (against documents with `isActive = 1`), `validateTotalesCuadran` (`total = importe_exento + importe_exonerado + Σ base_gravada + Σ impuesto`, compared **in cents** with a tolerance of 1, never in floats). `documento_aduanero`/`archivo_url` are not accepted here (stripped, stay `NULL`). `impuestos` may be empty or absent; two rows with the same `tarifa` are a DTO 400. `lotes: []` is a 400. Dates use `fechaISO('La fecha de emision')`.

**`GET /documentos-fiscales`** — active documents, 19 fields: the header without the annulment trio, plus `cliente` and `cliente_rtn` (`INNER JOIN clientes`) and `pais` (the `codigo_pais`). **No taxes, no lotes.** Three optional filters with the spec 16 convention (`cliente_id`, `desde`, `hasta`; never a 400). `hasta` is a bare `<=`, **without** the `DATE_ADD` of the `pesajes` filters, because `fecha_emision` is a `DATE`. Ordered `fecha_emision` DESC **then `id` DESC** (a date without time guarantees ties). No pagination.

**`PATCH /documentos-fiscales/:id/completar`** — the only write besides spec 41's edit that updates a field that is neither audit nor lifecycle; it exists because the customs document closes *after* the invoice. Body `documento_aduanero` and `archivo_url`, both optional, **at least one** (DTO 400). Writes only the ones sent and only if the column is `NULL`; an already written field answers 400, and the other can be completed separately. No timestamp, no `userId`. `{ ok, msg }`. 400 if the document does not exist or is annulled. Everything else is corrected by annulling and registering again, never by an `UPDATE`.

**`PATCH /documentos-fiscales/:id/anular`** — logical annulment: `isActive = 0`, `motivo_anulacion`, `anulado_por`, `anulado_en = NOW()`. Body `motivo`, 5–255; `{ ok, msg }`. 400 if missing or already annulled. **Does not delete the child rows**, and from that come two asymmetric consequences — the least obvious thing in the module: it **frees the lotes** for invoicing again (`validateLotesNoFacturados` filters `isActive = 1`) but **never frees the number** (the `UNIQUE` filters nothing). Irreversible; the document leaves the listing and stays visible in the detail.

**`GET /documentos-fiscales/:id`** — the endpoint the module exists for. `{ ok, msg, documento }`: header, client, the country as a nested object (`codigo_pais`, `etiqueta_autorizacion`, `etiqueta_identificacion`, so the front labels `CAI` or `NIT` without knowing the country), `impuestos`, `lotes` and, per lote, the physical traceability — `nombre_lote`, `variedad_o_talla`, `producto`, `unidad_medida`, `unidad_medida_facturada`, `aprobado_por`/`finalizado_por` resolved to `complete_name` with their dates, the **count of active weigh-ins** and the **sum of their `peso_neto`**: the declared number and the physical weight behind it in one response. **Four queries**, not one per lote (one `GROUP BY lote_id`). **Returns the document even if annulled**, with `isActive: 0`, `motivo_anulacion` and who annulled it — the opposite of `GET /pesajes/:id`, because a read that exists to audit cannot hide what was annulled. **404** if the id does not exist; no 400 by state. The client is read by `JOIN`, so **editing a client's `nombre` (spec 41) renames it on every registered invoice** — accepted risk; there is no snapshot.

**`@Get(':id')` is declared last in `DocumentosFiscalesController`** (with a comment on the handler). Move it up and `GET /documentos-fiscales` answers the detail with a `ParseIntPipe` 400.

## A second country

Its own spec, and it drags three things: `clientes.pais_id` with its FK, the uniqueness of `clientes.rtn` moving from global to `(pais_id, rtn)`, and `POST /clientes` receiving the country — plus `GET /catalogos/paises`, declined while one active row makes the selector pointless. Until then the global rule is correct.

## Deferred

Emitting anything fiscal (emitter/point-of-sale/authorization tables, correlativos with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`, authorization rotation, tax calculation, total in words, PDF); electronic invoicing and transmission to a tax authority; a CRUD for `paises_config` (a new country is a business decision, not a screen); `GET /documentos-fiscales/resumen-isv` (serves *declaring*, not *auditing*); file upload behind `archivo_url`; credit/debit notes as a flow (`tipo_documento` accepts them as values only); receivables, payments, balances; prices; validating `cantidad` against the lote's summed `peso_neto` (an invoice may cover part of a lote — reconciling is the auditor's job); undoing an annulment or editing a registered document; a snapshot of the client's name and RTN (and `direccion_fiscal`); pagination of the listing; **a listing of finalized lotes pending invoicing** — `finalizado_por IS NOT NULL` with no row in `documento_fiscal_lote` of an active document — which is what detects a lote orphaned after an annulment nobody followed up.
